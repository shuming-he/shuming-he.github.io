Same bag. Three markets.

I compared what 15 luxury fashion houses and watchmakers charge for the same bags and watches in the United States, Canada and mainland China. Pick a brand to see every price, converted to one currency with tax included, and where each piece is cheapest.

Method

From product pages to a buying decision

Every number goes through the same five-step pipeline, so a price in Toronto and a price in Shanghai are compared fairly.

  1. Scrape

    Collect list prices from each brand's official US, Canada and China sites with requests and BeautifulSoup, timestamping every row.

  2. Match

    Link the same model across markets by style number, size and material.

  3. Normalize

    Convert CAD and CNY to USD. Add US sales tax and Canadian HST; China prices already include 13% VAT.

  4. Test

    Paired t-tests on log price ratios and a regression of the China premium on price tier.

  5. Recommend

    For each product, rank the three markets and report the cheapest one and the saving.
